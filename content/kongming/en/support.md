# Technical Support

Kongming Tools offers everyday calculators, code scanning and OCR, image processing, document conversion, a watermark camera, date tools, and unit conversion. Contact us if you need help or have suggestions.

## Contact support {#support-email}

Email: [apw_service@163.com](mailto:apw_service@163.com).

Describe the affected tool, steps to reproduce the issue, expected outcome, and actual outcome. Include the app version, device model, and iOS version. If useful, attach a screenshot with personal information hidden or the error message. We will investigate using the information provided; handling time depends on the complexity of the issue.

In-app Feedback initially saves a local draft. After choosing Share Feedback, you must select a destination such as email and send it. Saving a draft alone does not submit feedback to us. You can also email us directly.

## Camera, Photos, and location {#permissions}

If you cannot take photos, scan codes, or save to Photos, check the app's camera or add-to-Photos permissions in iOS Settings and make sure sufficient storage is available. The system photo picker provides only selected items. Photos or Live Photos stored in iCloud may need a network download.

Entering the watermark camera requests location permission while in use. Photos omit location if permission is denied or location lookup fails; photography and saving remain available. Check system Location Services and app permissions. Place-name lookup uses Apple services and may depend on network and location conditions.

## Recognition, conversion, and export {#processing}

For inaccurate OCR or code recognition, use a clear, well-lit image and avoid tilt, glare, and obstructions. Verify recognized content. If a calculation seems incorrect, check the inputs, units, and calculation method.

If document conversion fails, make sure the source file is not encrypted or damaged and uses a supported format. Try a smaller file, or export a PDF from the source application first. PDF to Word or Excel primarily extracts text; PDF to PowerPoint creates page images. Original layouts, tables, and formulas may not be reconstructed. Review the preview first.

Use system sharing to save exports to Photos, Files, or another destination. Temporary files may be cleared by the system, so keep important originals and independent backups. Live Photo to GIF does not retain audio. Wait for cloud content to finish downloading before processing it.

## Purchases and restoration {#purchases}

If a purchase or product loading fails, check your network connection and App Store account, then retry. If a purchase is not reflected in your membership status, use the Apple account used for the purchase and select Restore Purchases on the membership screen. If the issue persists, email the error message and necessary purchase details with sensitive information hidden.

Manage or cancel auto-renewable subscriptions in iOS Settings under your Apple account's Subscriptions section. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. See [Apple subscription support](https://support.apple.com/118428). Request eligible refunds through [Apple's refund page](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/); approval depends on Apple's applicable rules and statutory rights.

## Local data and privacy requests {#data}

Favorites, countdowns, templates, preferences, and feedback drafts are stored locally. We cannot remotely access or recover content held only on your device. Save important files before uninstalling, and manage system backups and exported copies separately.

Do not email passwords, card numbers, complete payment details, Wi-Fi passwords, or unnecessary sensitive documents. To request access, correction, or deletion of information actually received by the developer through email, contact the address above. We handle requests under applicable law.
