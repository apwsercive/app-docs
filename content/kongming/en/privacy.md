# Privacy Policy

## Scope {#section-1}

This Privacy Policy covers the Kongming Tools iOS app, including calculators, scanning, image and document conversion, date tools, watermark photography, and membership purchases and restoration. It describes the current version. See the contact section for the operator and contact details.

## On-device processing and collection {#section-2}

No app account is required. Calculator inputs, QR and barcode contents, selected images and files, recognized text, document scans, and generated images and PDFs are processed on your device. The app does not upload this content to developer servers.

The current version has no developer-operated content backend, advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs. It does not collect an advertising identifier for tracking. Apple services and external services you choose may use the network, as described below.

## Information stored on your device {#section-3}

Favorites, tool and group order, recent tools, countdowns, language, theme, appearance, haptics, launch preferences, and feedback drafts are stored locally. The app also records the first-launch date to determine when to show a startup membership offer. Watermark templates, text, styles, and colors are also stored locally.

Imported images, previews, and exported files may remain in the app sandbox or temporary directory. The system may remove temporary files; closing a screen does not guarantee immediate deletion. Save important exports separately. Device backups, restoration, and cloud file providers depend on your system settings and the relevant services.

## Permissions and your choices {#section-4}

Camera: accessed when you choose to take a photo, scan a code, or scan a document. Recognition happens on your device. You can revoke permission in iOS Settings.

Photos: the system picker provides selected images and Live Photos without requiring full library access. Saving a photo or GIF to Photos requests add-only permission. Apple may download selected iCloud content to the device.

Files: the system picker provides selected files, with access used as needed while reading. Sources may include iCloud or another file provider you configured.

Calendar: adding a countdown opens the system event editor for you to review and save. The app does not read calendar content for analytics.

Sharing: content leaves the app only through actions you choose, such as system sharing, copying, or opening a link. The recipient handles it under its own policies. Refusing a permission does not prevent unrelated tools from working.

## Watermark camera and location {#location}

When you enter the watermark camera, the app requests location permission while in use. If authorized, it obtains the current location and uses Apple MapKit or Core Location reverse geocoding. This may send location and request-related information to Apple to resolve a place name, under Apple’s service policies. The app does not continuously track location in the background or upload it to developer servers.

You can deny or revoke location permission in iOS Settings. Photos omit location if permission is denied or lookup fails, and photography and saving remain available. Templates can render time, a place name, or coordinates as visible text. Exported watermark JPEGs omit original EXIF/GPS metadata, but visible location text may still reveal your location. Review it before sharing.

## Membership, payment, and Apple services {#section-5}

Membership purchases, automatic renewal, and restoration use Apple StoreKit and the App Store. The app reads and verifies product and entitlement information on the device to display membership status. It does not receive your card number, complete payment details, or Apple account password.

Apple may process account, transaction, and diagnostic data under its policies and your system settings. Purchases and restoration may require a network connection. Scanned content is not uploaded to a developer backend for entitlement verification.

## Retention, deletion, and control {#section-6}

You can change preferences, remove supported records and favorites, and save results using system sharing. Removing local content or uninstalling the app removes the corresponding app data; manage system backups and files exported elsewhere separately.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings. There is no developer-operated account to delete. The developer cannot remotely access, recover, or delete content held only on your device.

## Third-party components and websites {#section-7}

Image editing uses the open-source ZLImageEditor component on your device. Scanning, recognition, file selection, calendar editing, and sharing use Apple platform capabilities. The current version includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDK.

When you open a policy page, Apple support, or a scanned URL, the website may process IP addresses, browser information, and access logs. This documentation site is hosted on GitHub Pages and adds no advertising, analytics scripts, third-party fonts, or tracking cookies. GitHub may still process basic access data to operate and secure the service and meet legal obligations. See the GitHub Privacy Statement linked below. External websites are outside the app’s on-device processing scope.

## Children, security, and changes {#section-8}

A parent or guardian should guide minors when using camera, file-sharing, or purchase features. The app does not require registration or identity documents. Birth dates entered voluntarily into age and date tools are used only for on-device calculations. Protect your device with system security controls and take care when sharing documents containing personal information.

We update this policy and its date when functionality or data practices change. New collection, permissions, or purposes will receive appropriate in-app explanations and authorization where required.

## Feedback and contact information {#feedback}

In-app feedback remains a local draft until you choose a sharing destination and send it. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments to handle your request, troubleshoot, and maintain legally necessary records; we delete or anonymize them when no longer needed. Do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information actually held by the developer. We verify and handle requests under applicable law.
