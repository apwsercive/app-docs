# YiSense Privacy Policy

## Scope {#scope}

This policy covers YiSense for iOS: daily readings, traditional time queries, I Ching study, casting methods, personal journals, iCloud sync and record export. It describes the first version. The app has no developer-operated accounts, advertising, behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking or AI chat service.

## On-device information {#local}

You choose whether to enter a question or save a reading. Casting inputs, coin outcomes, dates, time zones, method versions, hexagram snapshots and personal notes are processed on your device and stored in its local database when saved. Reading the study library does not require personal questions or birth information.

Language, accent color, appearance, haptic preferences and reading expansion state are stored locally. These preferences do not request location, contacts or microphone access. Device time and time zone settings are used without collecting your geographic location.

## Additional record details and images {#attachments}

Records can be marked for yourself or someone else and include a name, question, category, manual interpretation, follow-up, outcome status, tags and notes. Only record information you are entitled to use, and keep it to what is necessary. Outcome status is your own annotation, not an automatic app judgment.

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker provides only the images you select without reading your entire library. Images are resized and re-encoded on device without copying original EXIF metadata, then synced as record attachments through iCloud. Visible image content may still be private; review it before saving. Cancelling an editor does not commit draft attachments. Removing saved attachments propagates after sync.

## iCloud sync {#icloud}

Saved records are first written locally and then mirrored through Apple CloudKit to the private database of the current Apple Account. Synced fields include identifiers, dates, questions, methods, original inputs, times and time zones, algorithm versions, hexagram snapshots, basic and advanced record fields, outcome annotations and image attachments. The developer operates no server receiving this personal text and does not obtain your Apple Account password through the app.

Sync depends on your iCloud account, network, storage quota and Apple's scheduling. You can save locally while offline or signed out; sync may resume when conditions permit. A completed sync activity does not guarantee that every device is up to date. Account switching and sign-out are handled by the system, and local or other-device copies may remain.

Do not save information you do not want to enter iCloud. You can manage the app's iCloud access and cloud data in system settings. These actions may affect local copies and sync; back up important records first. Avoid entering passwords, payment details, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information about yourself or others.

## Export and deletion {#control}

Export creates a JSON file containing records only when you request it. You choose its destination in the system file interface, which may include local storage, iCloud Drive or a third-party provider governed by its own policy. Deletions propagate to other devices after successful sync. Offline devices, exported files and system backups may retain copies requiring separate management.

Uninstalling the app does not delete iCloud data. There is no developer-operated account to close. The developer cannot view, restore or delete local records or private iCloud content that the developer does not hold.

## External links and Apple services {#external}

Classical texts and these policies are bundled for offline reading. Opening a source, email or other external link invokes a system application or website that may process your IP address, browser information and access logs. iCloud and system diagnostics are governed by Apple's policies and your system settings.

## Security and updates {#updates}

Protect your device with a passcode and system security features. Review personal content before sharing or exporting. Minors should use the app with guardian guidance. We will update this dated policy when features or information handling change and provide relevant explanations when needed.
