# YiSense Technical Support

## Contact {#1}

Email [apw_service@163.com](mailto:apw_service@163.com) with the app version, device model, iOS version, steps, expected outcome and actual outcome. Hide names, questions, photos and private information in screenshots. Do not send Apple passwords, identity documents or complete private journals. Handling time depends on the issue.

## Records and iCloud {#2}

Records save locally before Apple schedules synchronisation. Check iCloud sign-in, network, available space and app settings. Recent sync activity does not confirm that all devices match. Offline creation can produce multiple entries. Export a backup before removing duplicates; do not uninstall or erase cloud data as a first troubleshooting step. We cannot remotely recover content held only on your device or private iCloud.

## Images and export {#3}

The camera requires your action and permission; the photo picker provides selected images only, which may need an iCloud download. Records allow up to eight images, resized to a longest edge of 1600 pixels and re-encoded. JSON export does not imply import or restore support. Exports include text records and Base64-encoded attachment images. Base64 is readable encoding, not encryption. Images are processed copies, not original-photo backups. Deleting a record also deletes its attachments and propagates after sync. Manage exports and system backups separately.

## Casting and reading {#4}

Check the method, inputs, device date and time zone. Number casting accepts three integers from 1 to 1,000,000. Methods and traditions differ. Daily reading cycles by date and is not a personal prediction. Classical texts remain in Chinese; the app supports Simplified and Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Malay and Thai. If membership is missing, use Restore Purchases with the purchasing Apple account. Manage cancellation separately in Apple account Subscriptions. Report source, rule or translation issues by email.
