## Rules and results {#calculation}

Each casting method explains its rules. Different methods and traditions can yield different results. Traditional time uses the device's local time and time zone, including system daylight-saving rules, changes the day at midnight and changes the lunar year on Lunar New Year's Day. It does not use apparent solar time or treat lunar month numbers as solar-term month pillars.

Results may be affected by device time, time zone, calendar range, inputs and algorithm version. Saved castings retain their inputs and version for review. Check the method, inputs and results before use. Daily readings rotate by date and are not personalized judgments about your circumstances.
