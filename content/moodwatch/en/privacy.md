# MoodWatch Privacy Policy

## Scope {#scope}

This policy describes how MoodWatch handles information in its iPhone app, Apple Watch app, and widgets, and what happens when you open the documentation website. The current version does not require a MoodWatch account or include AI chat, advertising, or cross-app tracking.

## Health data and its uses {#health}

With your permission, MoodWatch can read the following types of data from Apple Health. Available data depends on your device, recorded samples, and the permissions you grant.

- Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability (HRV), and heartbeat series support stress and recovery estimates, cardiovascular summaries, and trends.
- Sleep analysis, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, and basal body temperature support sleep summaries, sleep vitals, and metrics.
- Workouts, steps, stand time, active and basal energy, VO₂ max, body fat percentage, time in daylight, and walking asymmetry support activity and body metrics.
- Date of birth, biological sex, sample times, and data sources support applicable estimates, date grouping, and source selection.

MoodWatch calculates stress, sleep, and body metrics on your device. It uses these results to display charts and summaries and to generate local notifications you enable. MoodWatch does not sell health data, use it for advertising, or send health records to a developer-operated server.

## On-device storage, Apple Watch, and iCloud {#storage}

MoodWatch stores preferences, sleep goals, data-source priorities, and display caches. The iPhone and Apple Watch apps can exchange limited health summaries using Apple WatchConnectivity. App Group storage makes summaries available to widgets on the relevant device. Summaries may include measurement times, stress, HRV, and sleep metrics.

If you use the journal, MoodWatch stores your mood, tags, notes, the time of the linked reading, and its HRV value. Journal entries are stored locally and sync through Apple’s iCloud key-value storage between devices using the same Apple Account. Some preferences use the same service. Sync depends on your device and Apple Account settings.

## Purchases, website visits, and support {#services}

Apple StoreKit handles membership purchases and restoration. MoodWatch uses product and transaction status to determine your access. Apple handles payment information.

When you open a policy or support page, your browser or the in-app web view connects to the documentation host. The host may receive technical information needed to serve the page, such as your IP address and browser details. MoodWatch does not include health readings, journal entries, or membership information in the policy URLs. If you send us a support email, we use your email address and the information you provide to respond.

## Managing permissions and data {#choices}

You can change health permissions in Apple Health or system settings and turn off notifications in system settings. Revoking permission limits future reads. Data already stored in app caches, journal entries, or synced storage may remain there. Uninstalling MoodWatch removes the local app installation and local app data. Apple Health records and information already synced to iCloud are managed through those services.

For questions about retention or requests for access, correction, or deletion, email [apw_service@163.com](mailto:apw_service@163.com). For help with app settings, see [Technical Support](support.md).
