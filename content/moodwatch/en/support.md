# MoodWatch Technical Support

## Missing metrics or sleep data {#health}

Check the relevant permissions in MoodWatch under **Settings → Health Data Access**, then check Apple Health for records on the date you selected. Some metrics depend on a compatible device or sensor. When multiple devices record the same night of sleep, check their sources and priorities under **Data Source Management**.

## Apple Watch, widgets, and notifications {#devices}

Check pairing and app installation under **Settings → Apple Watch**. The system schedules watch and widget updates, so check the timestamp when comparing values. For notifications, check both system permissions and the notification preferences in MoodWatch.

## Restoring purchases {#purchase}

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and choose the restore option on the membership screen. Apple handles subscription management, cancellation, and refunds.

## Contact support {#help}

Email [apw_service@163.com](mailto:apw_service@163.com). Include the app version, device, system version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Remove health and other personal information from screenshots before sending them. See also the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) and [Terms of Service](terms.md).
