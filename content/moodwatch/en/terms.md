# MoodWatch Terms of Service

## Scope {#scope}

MoodWatch provides stress, sleep, sleep vitals, body metrics, historical trends, journaling, and notifications across its iPhone app, Apple Watch app, and widgets. Availability depends on supported devices, software, permissions, recorded data, and membership. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) for details on data handling.

## Health information and its limits {#wellness}

Stress, sleep quality, and body age are estimates based on available data and are intended for general wellness and lifestyle use. Personal reference ranges compare your recent observations with your own history. MoodWatch is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or clinical monitoring. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns. For emergencies, contact local emergency services.

Apple Health data, sensor readings, and sync can be incomplete or delayed. Widgets, watch summaries, and notifications can show earlier information or fail to update promptly. Check the timestamp and data source.

## Membership and purchases {#purchases}

See the in-app offer and the Apple purchase confirmation for available plans, prices, billing periods, and benefits. Apple processes purchases and restores. Auto-renewable subscriptions renew under the terms displayed at purchase. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings and request refunds through Apple. Uninstalling MoodWatch does not cancel your subscription.

## Use and third-party services {#use}

Use MoodWatch lawfully and respect others’ privacy and rights. You can manage health and notification permissions through system settings or stop using the app at any time. Apple Health, iCloud, StoreKit, and third-party websites you open have their own terms and privacy practices. The app may change as system capabilities and the product evolve. These terms do not limit any consumer rights that apply under law.
